# Blastoucan production brief

Owner-supplied Cannon Beak creature: vivid-blue/cream body, teal and yellow wing feather bands, three blue/yellow/red crest feathers, charcoal bird feet and striped red/orange/yellow feather tail. Its identifying feature is a huge horizontal hollow cannon beak with a blue base, yellow mid-band, red-orange muzzle and clearly dark circular bore. Preserve the larger upright body and sharp spread wing silhouette; do not replace its cannon with Parrocket's conical beak. Reference height 1.25 m and weight 24.6 kg are design notes.

Four owner references were supplied inline in chat: design board, 4×5 combat sheet and two directional sheets. Original reference bytes are not exposed in the workspace and must not be described as retained originals. Image generation can use the inline references; retain all produced originals, exact prompts and reviewed revisions. Internal identity/technical review is separate from owner taste approval.

Prepare optional portrait, twenty combat poses (idle, attack, hit, faint, capture × four) and forty follower poses (north, northeast, east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest × idle/four walks). Correct repeated north/south and diagonal/profile anatomy in the references rather than copying mislabeled rows. North shows the back, south faces the viewer with round cannon bore, west is profile, northwest is rear-left; all eight must be genuinely distinct. Planted idle, alternating bird feet and natural wing/tail counter-motion; keep every complete cannon/crest/tail isolated with broad transparent source gutters. Use one scale and ground baseline per sheet, and review at renderer widths 37/115.

Combat keeps a left-facing upright identity; attack anticipates cannon recoil, fires, recoils and settles. Hit reactions show recovery, faint gradually folds wings and rests, capture reactions stay readable without a pre-drawn capture orb. Existing Fire material effects provide the actual elemental projectile. Do not add a ranged-distance mechanic, second combat type, scouting AI, speed stat or inferred evolution. The optional pack definition uses primary Fire and existing move rules; Air remains a reference trait. Current eighteen-species roster and maps stay unchanged.
