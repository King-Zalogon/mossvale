# Coglet reusable creature resource

Coglet is the owner-supplied small toy-like machine from two inline concept/movement boards. Preserve its rounded cream/brass casing and paired cyan lenses on the forward face only. Rear views hide both lenses; profiles show the near lens at the leading edge. Its vertical dorsal gear lies in the front-to-rear plane, like a bicycle wheel, with a transverse axle: front/rear views show the narrow rim, flanks show the flat disc and diagonals show an oblique face. Spinning changes tooth phase, never the mounting plane. Four fixed corner attachments share the same charcoal joint, short segment and brass wedge foot. The brass-centered dark flank housing stays above the rear leg, behind the lenses. Its personality is curious, methodical and persistent.

The optional assets are `creature-coglet`, `creature-coglet-combat` and `creature-coglet-follower`: a portrait, twenty battle poses (idle, gear-powered lunge, hit/recovery, faint, capture reactions × four) and forty map poses (eight directions × planted idle/four walks). Open `dist/coglet-preview.html` through a local server to inspect all poses using shared renderers at 115 px battle and 37 px map width. The isolated preview does not modify game saves or the active roster.
