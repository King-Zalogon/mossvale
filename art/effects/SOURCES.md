# Elemental attack effects — #173

Design was committed at `b8982a1` in [the twelve-element brief](../../docs/ELEMENTAL_ATTACKS.md) before generating any effects. All four original sheets and their spacing revisions were produced with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool on 2026-10-11. Exact prompts are retained in [generation.json](generation.json). The tool did not expose a model version, seed or mask. No Pokémon or Saint11 artwork/code is incorporated; those references informed anticipation, directional travel and contact timing only.

Each sheet has three materials and six poses: charge, launch, travel A, travel B, impact, dissolve. Original candidates were rejected for insufficient clear spacing. Referenced image edits produced the retained `*-spaced.png` sheets. Immutable original and edited PNG bytes are archived in bounded chunks through `art/characters/source-archive.json`; `source_archive.source_bytes` reconstructs and checks their SHA-256 hashes.

`python3 art/effects/export.py` extracts complete poses using reviewed row boundaries, rejects boundary contact and thresholds low-alpha generator glow at 128. One shared nearest-neighbor scale per material fits all six poses within 96 pixels. Colors above the alpha threshold are preserved. The 128-pixel cells have at least 16 pixels of horizontal padding and a common six-pixel bottom inset. Per-pose vertical center pivots keep differently shaped effects centered in the battle scene without changing the bottom-center asset contract. The editable atlas and shipped atlas are byte-identical RGBA pixels; `--check` verifies reproducibility.

Review the material silhouettes on the dark battle background at 108-pixel canvas size, including mirrored travel, and at the compact 64-pixel minimum. Sharp leaves, a clear splash, solid rocks, a tapered fireball, ice spears, a branching bolt, open wind curls, rounded spores, broad petals, opaque mud, granular sand and crystalline frost remain distinct by shape/material. Production review is internal; it does not claim owner taste approval. Creature identity sprites are unchanged.
