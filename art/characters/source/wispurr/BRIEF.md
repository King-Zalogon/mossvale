# Wispurr resource production brief

Owner references: two inline Wispurr concept/movement boards. Original binaries are unavailable locally; generated adaptations are retained as derived artwork, not original reference bytes.

Preserve a small dark-violet flame-shaped floating spirit, pale cream oval mask, large indigo eyes with cyan glints and one indigo forehead mark. Two pointed upward head wisps tipped in cyan flames, small arm-like side wisps, and attached blue/cyan curling lower flame/tail. Shy, sensitive and clever. No legs, animal fur, wings, dorsal gear, extra faces or detached particles. Clear simple pixel clusters in violet/indigo, blue, cream, dark gray and cyan.

Deliver transparent portrait; eight anatomically rotated isometric views (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW), calm neutral hover plus four travel-hover phases each. Front mask visible south; north rear silhouette has no mask; side profiles differ from rear diagonals. Art expresses gentle floating through attached flame curl/length and body tilt, maintaining a stable virtual ground anchor; movement still uses existing collision rules. Use existing follower idle/walk slot IDs for compatibility, with authored hover poses instead of footsteps.

Combat: four poses each idle, anticipation/flame-body lunge/recovery, hit/brace recovery, faint/collapsed resting wisp, capture/cooperative calm. Final faint rests lowered, mask eyes dim/closed. Preserve identity through every pose. No pre-drawn capture orb, unrelated sparks or symbols. Broad source gaps, complete head wisps/tails and at least four transparent output edge pixels. Inspect using shared renderer at 37px map / 115px battle width.

Optional importable resource only. Air is a provisional primary type, using existing gust-spiral, because the current engine has no Spirit type. Spirit Starter is descriptive: no new type, starter choice, ghost immunities, flight collision, emotional AI or evolution is implied. Future authors review type/stats/balance before import. Current maps/roster/save identities unchanged.
